The app icon comes from the [upstream LearnHarness repository](https://github.com/amosblomqvist/learnharness), and the learning engine and web interface this app displays come from there too, served by your own LearnHarness server. **LearnHarness for Android** is an independent Android adaptation and packaging of that project, maintained by [Vyas Devgna](https://github.com/vyas-devgna). It is not an official upstream release, and the upstream developer has not endorsed it.

The server-shell version (1.0.0 and later) replaces the bundled on-phone server with a WebView for a self-hosted server. Its web-side Android changes were moved into the [shouryadixitisverycool/learnharness](https://github.com/shouryadixitisverycool/learnharness) fork.
